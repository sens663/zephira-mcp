# Zephira company intelligence

Use Zephira when the user asks to research company identity, officers, shareholders, group relationships or financial statements.
Resolve a company with search_entities using the user's jurisdiction and a name or identifier. If the jurisdiction or company match is ambiguous, ask the user to choose before retrieving detailed records.
Preserve returned source links, timestamps and modelled-data labels. Coverage varies by company and jurisdiction; do not treat missing fields as proof that a fact does not exist. All six tools are read-only. Data requests consume the user's Zephira workspace allowance.
